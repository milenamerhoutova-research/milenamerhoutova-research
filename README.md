### Milena Merhoutova — Research Portfolio Website

Welcome to the repository for my personal research portfolio website, hosted live via GitHub Pages. This website acts as a centralized hub to showcase my academic background, active research projects, publications, and professional updates. 

🌐 **Live Website:** [milenamerhoutova-research.github.io/milenamerhoutova-research/](https://milenamerhoutova-research.github.io/milenamerhoutova-research/) 

### 📋 Features & Sections

The website is designed to be highly readable, responsive, and easy to navigate for colleagues, collaborators, and recruiters. It includes: 

* **About Me:** An overview of my academic background, core research interests, and expertise.
* **Research Projects:** Detailed summaries of active and past research initiatives.
* **Publications & Preprints:** A list of papers, journal articles, and conference contributions with links to full texts or DOIs.
* **CV / Resume:** A clean layout highlighting my education, teaching experience, and technical skill set.
* **Contact & Socials:** Channels to connect with me (Email, LinkedIn, Google Scholar, etc.).
